Terms

How we play together

Leapstone is a free app to play Chinese checkers and Halma, for everyone, also with friends. Some online opponents may be computer players. By choosing a player name you agree to these rules.

No tolerance

There is no place in Leapstone for content or behaviour that is:

  • rude, abusive or obscene;
  • hateful, or aimed at a person’s origin, religion, gender, sexuality, disability or age;
  • threatening, bullying or intimidating;
  • sexual, or encouraging violence or self-harm.

This goes for your player name and for every message you write in the chat of a game. Leapstone refuses messages and player names with rude words. Do not try to get around that.

Report and block

You are in control of what you see.

Report
Tap or long-press a message from another player and choose Report. We keep a copy of the message and look at it.
Block
Choose Block on the same menu. You no longer see messages from that player, and you cannot join the same game. You can unblock under Online, Account, Blocked players.

What we do

If a player breaks these rules, we can hide the message and remove the player. A removed player can no longer chat, start games or join games. Games that are already running go on.

Your account

You can delete your account at any time. See the privacy policy for the steps and for what we store.

Nederlands

Met het kiezen van een spelersnaam ga je akkoord met deze regels. Sommige online tegenstanders kunnen computerspelers zijn.

  • Geen tolerantie: geen grove, haatdragende, bedreigende of intimiderende inhoud, in je spelersnaam en in de chat. Leapstone weigert berichten en spelersnamen met grove woorden.
  • Rapporteren: tik op een bericht van een andere speler en kies Report. Wij bewaren een kopie en bekijken het.
  • Blokkeren: kies Block. Je ziet dan geen berichten meer van die speler en je kunt niet in hetzelfde potje spelen. Deblokkeren kan onder Online, Account, Blocked players.
  • Weren: wie de regels breekt, kan niet meer chatten, geen potjes starten en niet meedoen. Lopende potjes gaan door.
