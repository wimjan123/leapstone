Chinese checkers rules: how to play

Chinese checkers is a game for 2, 3, 4 or 6 players. The goal is to move all 10 of your pieces across the star board to the opposite point before anyone else. On your turn you move one piece, by a step to an empty spot next to it or by jumping over a piece to the empty spot behind it, and you may keep jumping. The first player to fill the opposite point wins.

The board

Chinese checkers is played on a star with six points. The board has 121 spots, and every point has 10 of them. Inside the board, each spot has six neighbors.

How the pieces start

Every player has 10 pieces and starts with them in one point of the star. The point across from yours is your goal.

  • 2 players: the two points across from each other.
  • 3 players: every other point. The three goals start empty.
  • 4 players: two pairs of points that sit next to each other. Every player has another player across.
  • 6 players: all six points.

Taking turns

Player 1 goes first. Then the turns go in order, Player 2, Player 3 and so on, and after the last player it is Player 1 again. In Leapstone the colors go with the numbers: red, blue, green, yellow, purple and silver.

On your turn you move one piece, once. Nothing is captured: pieces stay on the board until their player resigns.

A step

Move a piece to an empty spot next to it.

A jump

Jump over one piece next to yours, of any color, and land on the empty spot right behind it, in a straight line. You can jump in any of the six directions, also backwards. Three short answers: can you move backwards?, can you jump your own piece? and can you jump over your opponent?

  • The spot you land on must be empty.
  • You cannot jump over two pieces in a row, or over an empty spot.
  • The piece you jump over stays where it is.

A chain of jumps

After a jump, the same piece may jump again, as many times as it can and in any direction. You decide where to stop. All the jumps together are one move.

  • In one move you cannot land on the same spot twice.
  • A move is one step, or one or more jumps. You cannot step after a jump.

The goal

You win by moving all 10 of your pieces into the point across from where you started. All 10 spots of that point must hold your own pieces. While a piece of another player sits there, you cannot finish.

You may land on any empty spot, also in the points of other players.

With 2, 3, 4 or 6 players

  • 2 players: the first player to fill the goal wins, and the game is over.
  • 3, 4 or 6 players: the first player to fill the goal wins. The others play on for the next places, and a player who is done is skipped. The game is over when only one player is still playing.

Resigning

When you resign, your pieces leave the board, so they do not block anyone. With 2 players, the other player wins. With more players the game goes on without you, and if everyone but one player resigns, the last player wins.

In an online game every move has a time limit. The player who starts the game sets it, and in a quick game it is 30 seconds. When your time runs out, your turn is skipped. If you miss 3 turns in a row, you resign. Games against the computer and on one phone have no time limit.

Tips

  • Build ladders. Put pieces in a line with one empty spot between them, pointing at your goal. One piece can then jump along the whole line in a single move.
  • Any piece is a ladder. You can jump over every color, so the pieces of the other players help you too. And your own ladders help them: look at what you leave behind.
  • Do not forget the stragglers. You need all 10 pieces home. A piece far behind, with nothing to jump over, moves one spot per turn. Bring it along while the other pieces are still around it.
  • Your start is their goal. With 2, 4 or 6 players, your start point is the goal of the player across from you, and they cannot finish while one of your pieces is still there. The same goes for you.

Questions

How many people can play Chinese checkers?

Two, three, four or six. Every player starts in one point of the star. With 2, 3 or 4 players, some points stay empty.

How many marbles does each player have?

Every player has 10 marbles, or pieces. The board has 121 spots: 10 in each of the six points and 61 in the middle.

Can you jump over your own pieces, or backwards?

Yes. You can jump over a piece of any color, your own too, in any of the six directions, also backwards. The piece you jump over stays where it is.

Do you capture pieces in Chinese checkers?

No. Nothing is captured. A piece only leaves the board when its player resigns.

Is Chinese checkers from China?

No. The game appeared in Germany in 1892 as Stern-Halma, based on the older game Halma. The name Chinese checkers comes from the United States, in 1928. Read more in Sternhalma.
