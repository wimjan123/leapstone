Can you move backwards in Chinese checkers?

Yes, you can move backwards in Chinese checkers: a piece may step or jump in any of its six directions, forwards, sideways or back.

Play now

The rule

A piece can step to any empty spot next to it, or jump over any piece next to it to the empty spot right behind it. Both work in all six directions. The rules do not name a front or a back, so a step or a jump that goes away from your goal is a move like any other.

The limits are the same in every direction: the spot you land on must be empty, and you cannot jump over an empty spot or over two pieces in a row. In a chain of jumps, a piece also never lands on a spot it already visited in that move, including the spot it started from.

A jump backwards can start a long move

A step backwards moves you away from your goal, so it rarely helps. A jump is different, because several jumps with one piece count as one move. A jump backwards can be the first link of a chain that ends far ahead of where the piece began.

Without the first jump, backwards, this piece could not reach the last spot in one move. All three jumps are one move, and the pieces it jumped over stay where they are.
